What is the mining step really doing?

When you press Space, the game hashes your block's text plus a counter (the nonce) with SHA-256 and checks whether the result starts with enough zeros. Each extra zero makes a win 16× less likely, so you mostly just keep trying.

Real Bitcoin does the same trick with an 80-byte block header, hashed twice, against a target trillions of times harder. This game's version is a simulation. See the Mining Lab for real headers, including Bitcoin's first block.
